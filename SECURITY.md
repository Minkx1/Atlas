## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take security seriously. If you discover a critical vulnerability, **please do not create a public issue**.
Instead, send a private message via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Minkx1/Atlas/security/advisories) or contact me directly at [zakalata.mark@gmail.com].

*We will aim to acknowledge receipt of the report within 48 hours and keep you updated on the resolution process.*
